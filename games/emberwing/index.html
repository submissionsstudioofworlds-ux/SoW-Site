The three breaths

Fire
Free and fast. Your bread and butter.
Frost
A three-shard cone that freezes what it hits. Frozen enemies take double damage — freeze, then shatter.
Storm
A piercing bolt that punches through a line of enemies and arcs to a nearby one. Expensive.

Staying alive

Frost, storm and surge all draw on the same ember meter, which refills on its own when you're not spending it — speed and firepower compete for the same resource. Rescue a drifting hatchling to refill it instantly and bank 1,000 points.

You earn an extra wing at 20,000 points (then every 40,000), and another for every five hatchlings you carry to safety — the pips beside your wing count track your progress. Clear the realm's kill quota and the Sky Leviathan comes for you. Four realms, then it all begins again — faster.
