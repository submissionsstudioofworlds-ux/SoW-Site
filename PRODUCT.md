# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step, deployed to Cloudflare Pages via GitHub auto-deploy

## Users

Three roughly equal audiences: (1) fans/viewers who land here after finding a Short on YouTube/TikTok and want to explore more of the studio's worlds, (2) aspiring AI filmmakers deciding whether to join the AI Cinematic Filmmaker Guild (Skool) or Discord, (3) partners/brands (other AI tool companies, collaborators) evaluating the studio's credibility and body of work.

## Product Purpose

studioofworlds.com is the central landing page/portfolio for The Studio of Worlds, an AI cinematic studio. It showcases the full range of work — original IP, myth reimaginings, and literary adaptations — and routes visitors to YouTube, social channels, Patreon, and the AI Cinematic Filmmaker Guild community.

## Positioning

Range across genres is the differentiator: equally strong original sci-fi/fantasy universes, myth reimaginings (Irish/Greek), and literary adaptations, rather than one narrow niche. Reinforced by official creative-partner status with PixVerse, ThankYou AI, and YouArt AI.

## Operating Context

Content is produced with AI video-generation tools (PixVerse, Kling, ThankYou AI, YouArt AI) and published as YouTube Shorts/videos, then showcased here. The Irish myth ("Tuatha") content currently lives on a separate YouTube channel and will fold into the main Studio of Worlds channel later. New character/world videos get added as they're produced; unfinished worlds show as "Coming Soon" cards with real concept art, never a placeholder video.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Static site, no backend/build step. All video embeds are real YouTube Shorts (`rel=0` to limit end-screen suggestions to the studio's own channels). All stats (subscriber/view counts) and partner names are real and must stay accurate — never fabricated. Email newsletter capture is a known future addition, intentionally deferred for now.

## Brand Commitments

Name: The Studio of Worlds (short: SoW). Voice: cinematic, epic, mythic. Type system: Cinzel (serif display) for headings, Literata (serif) for body. Current visual system: black background, white text, gold accent. Creative partners to credit: PixVerse, ThankYou AI, YouArt AI.

## Evidence on Hand

Real, verified as of initial build: 10K+ YouTube subscribers, 139K+ views, 69 shorts and growing. Full roster of original IP, myth, and classics titles with real video links (see index.html). Social links: YouTube, TikTok, X, LinkedIn, Patreon, portfolio (begen.space), Skool guild, Discord.

## Product Principles

- Never fabricate stats, testimonials, or claims — only show what's real and current.
- Every world gets equal visual treatment regardless of genre; range is the pitch, not a hierarchy.
- Unfinished work is marked "Coming Soon" honestly rather than hidden or faked.
- Keep the stack simple (static, no build) so updates stay fast and low-risk.
